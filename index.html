SCORING METHOD

Likelihood × Impact

  • 1–4 Low

02 / FRAMEWORK

Five-part security lifecycle

Use these functions to organize an SME's security controls and improvement plan.

01

Identify

Inventory assets, data, users, suppliers and business processes. Understand what matters most.

02

Protect

Apply MFA, secure configurations, backups, access controls, patching and employee awareness.

03

Detect

Monitor accounts, endpoints and networks for suspicious activity and unusual behavior.

04

Respond

Define incident roles, communication steps, containment actions and evidence handling.

05

Recover

Restore services, validate backups, communicate with stakeholders and improve after incidents.

Priority actionMFA + least privilege + backups
